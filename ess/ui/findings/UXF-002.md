The axes table of a variant sits in the narrow column. Its content is 318 px wide in a frame of
230 px at 1366x768 and 208 px at 1280x720. At 1366x768 the action reads "Switch to t"; at 1280x720
it reads "Switch" and its centre is under the frame's edge, so the click of the switch journey does
not land. The two tables of the wide column scroll sideways too (1024 px and 880 px of content in
760 px), cut inside their JSON columns (UXF-005).

Heuristic 3 (nothing required hidden): fail.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (switch-axis), confidence 2 (the trial, the audit and the
heuristic review agree), cost M. Priority 0.82.

Fix in one line: give the axes table the wide column, or a short action label, so no table of the
page scrolls sideways at the gating viewports.
