Measured in R-01 at the gating viewports, both themes:

- 56.4 % of the text runs were under 14 px. The body is 15.2 px; under 14 px were the column
  headings (10.9 px), the navigation's group headings (10.6 px), badges and record labels (11.4 px),
  form labels and tag suggestions (11.9 px), section titles (12.2 px).
- The badges carry the state: Running, Stopped, Proposed, pass, fail, violated, Insufficient. They
  are the key data of every page and were drawn at 11.4 px.
- Light theme, text on its badge: warning 3.69:1 ("Insufficient"), success 3.72:1 ("pass",
  "Computed", "Ended", the "Live" badge), info 3.95:1 ("Running", "Proposed"), danger 4.07:1
  ("fail", "violated"). The bar for text this small is 4.5:1.
- Dark theme: the navigation's group headings, 10.6 px at 4.33:1.

Contrast was computed only for text on a plain colour; text on a gradient layer was not measured.
axe-core did not run.

Heuristic 15 (readable in every theme, key data at 14 px or more): fail.

Triage: frequency 11 of 11 scenarios, confidence 2 (the audit and the heuristic review agree),
cost S. Priority 6.00.

Fix in one line: raise the theme's small sizes to 14 px for badges and labels and darken the four
badge text colours.

## Closed in R-02

In `themes/studio.css`:

- One size for all small text, `--uxui-text-small: 0.925rem` (14.06 px at the root's 15.2 px). It
  sets the generator's `--ui-size-text-small`, `--ui-size-text-tiny` and `--ui-type-caption-size`,
  and the theme's own sizes of the navigation's group headings, the section titles, the column
  headings and the theme toggle. The column headings are no longer upper case, so they are about as
  wide as before.
- The text colour of a toned badge is set per theme (`--ui-tone-info-text`, `-success-`,
  `-warning-`, `-danger-`). Light: #2a57b8, #146b45, #8a5300, #ad2f3d, which give 5.56:1, 5.60:1,
  5.56:1 and 5.23:1 on their badge fills. Dark keeps the colours it had. The "Live" badge takes the
  success text colour.
- Dark theme: the navigation's group headings are #8b90b3, 6.24:1 on the sidebar.
- To keep the pages in their viewports with the larger text: table cells 0.45rem above and below
  (0.6rem before) and 0.4rem left and right (0.5rem), the page 1.25rem above and below (1.5rem).

The round's declarations (`ux-browser-checks/1`, the render declaration) hold no threshold on text,
so the bars are a file of the round: `ess/ui/rounds/studio.audit.json`, read by `summary.mjs`.
A round stays a measurement: a missed bar is printed and counted, and the command does not fail.
