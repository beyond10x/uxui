strength, is red for "hard" and blue for "soft"; the second, the verdict, is green for "pass" and
red for "violated". A hard rule that passed shows red beside green. Red means "failed" everywhere
else in the studio.

Smaller breaks of the same kind: "Running" and "Proposed" share blue; states are capitalised
(Running, Computed) and verdicts are not (pass, fail, violated).
