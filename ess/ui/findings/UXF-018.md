  a letter is typed. It does not say what it searches: one field of the row (the name of a target,
  the id of a campaign, the digest of a promotion, the note of a judgement).
- Beside the search of Targets stands a choice that reads "Select…" with no label. It picks the
  base document.
- "Host slot" and "Trade-off" are yes-or-no facts drawn as checkboxes of 13 px that cannot be
  changed. They look like controls.
