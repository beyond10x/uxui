The live climb is 1283 px of content in a 768 px viewport: five sections stacked in the wide
column. Scrolled to the step table or to "Frontier now", the header with Stop and the connection
state, and the summary with the campaign state, are out of view. The document declares Stop and the
state as always visible. While the connection is lost, nothing in view says so.

The start form scrolls too (1084 px): its submit button is below the fold (UXF-012).

Heuristics 3 (nothing required hidden) and 2 (state in words): fail and partial.

Triage: frequency 2 of 11 scenarios (live-climb, start-campaign), confidence 2 (the page checks,
the audit and the trial's looks agree), cost M. Priority 1.64.

Fix in one line: keep the header and the campaign state in view while the page scrolls, or fit the
climb into the viewport.

## Closed in R-03, by the yes side of the round's design decision

2026-11-27 (R-03).
