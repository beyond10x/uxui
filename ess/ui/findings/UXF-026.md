The live climb is 1249 px of content in a viewport of 768 px or 720 px: the KPIs, the chart of the
best value, the progress chart, the step table and "Frontier now" stand under each other in the
main column. As the page opens, the two charts are in view; the step table and the frontier are
not, and the trial's driver scrolls once per run to reach them.

Since R-03 the page keeps its header and its summary in view while it scrolls (UXF-004, closed by
the yes side of `ess/ui/rounds/R-03/decision.md`), so nothing the document declares always visible
is lost. What is left is this: gate 1 (no scroll) fails on the page, and a person who watches the
steps does not see the charts.

Severity: S2, not S1. R-01 read S1 as "a hard gate fails or a scenario cannot be completed", and
gate 1 does fail here. But R-01 gave S1 on gate 1 only where something required was out of reach;
the start form, which scrolls as well, is S2 (UXF-012). The owner may read it otherwise.

Heuristic 3 (nothing required hidden): partial.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (live-climb), confidence 2 (the audit and the trial's look
agree), cost M. Priority 0.27. Fitting the climb into the viewport is a layout change: it takes a
design decision.

Fix in one line: fit the climb into the viewport, for one by showing one chart at a time or the
step table beside the charts.
