Every field declared `as: map` or `as: list` reaches the React app as JSON text: the budget, the
first route, the diff, what gets worse and better, the gate requirement verdicts, the KPI values,
the evaluation values, the findings of a rule. The terminal renderer prints the same values as
"max_variants: 400, max_wall_time_s: 3600".

The JSON is also what makes the tables wide (UXF-001, UXF-002) and runs past its card: the budget
on the live climb at every viewport, the first route of a variant at 1280x720 and 960x1080.

This is the stock generator's rendering and a known gap (`docs/ess-gaps.md`, "A list of records as
text"). The studio cannot change it; under triage rule 5 it is a known loss. The studio's handling
of it is in UXF-001 and UXF-002: do not put these values into table columns.

Heuristic 7 (words the user can say): fail.

Triage: frequency 6 of 11 scenarios (live-climb, owner-trade-off, switch-axis, compare-and-judge,
compare-without-pick, propose-promotion), confidence 2, cost L. Priority 0.82. Known loss.

## R-03: the decision of a promotion reads in words; the finding stays open

Checked in this round, with ess 0.99.2, what the document and the theme can do.

No `as` of a field draws a list of records or a map as words in the generated app: its
`formatValue` joins the entries of a list with ", " and writes every entry that is an object as
JSON, and `as: map` does the same with the whole object. What the app does do: a field's path with
a dot reads a member, and through a list it reads that member of every entry. `losses.requirement_id`
gives `attention.glance_cost`, `gate_requirement_verdicts.verdict` gives `pass, fail, pass`.

The terminal renderer reads the same path differently. Through an object it reads the member
(`diff.changed` prints `pages/settings/layout`); through a list it prints nothing. And it prints
the list itself as words already (`requirement_id: attention.glance_cost, reference: 2,
candidate: 3`).

So the section "decision" of a promotion, and the record of the dialog that accepts its trade-off,
declare each list twice (`ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml:385` to `:395`, `:419` to `:426`):

- `losses`, `gains` and `gate_requirement_verdicts` as before: the terminal prints them, the test
  of `ess/ui/tests/studio.yaml` and the journey of `ess/ui/studio.uxui.yaml` name them, and
  `themes/studio.css` does not draw them in the React app;
- one field per member (`losses.requirement_id`, `losses.reference`, `losses.candidate`, the same
  of `gains`, and the requirement and the verdict of `gate_requirement_verdicts`): the React app
  draws them, in rows of three, and the terminal prints nothing for them.

Red, the round on the document and theme of R-02 (`R-03/before.round.json`), the expectation of
step 4 of `owner-trade-off` in each of its 4 runs:

```
expect {"path":"pages/promotions.detail/sections/decision","not_text":"{\""}
text   GATE DECISION FROM RATCHET gate fail Trade-off gate policy revision web-ux@3 Gets worse {"requirement_id":"attention.glance_cost","reference":2,"candidate":3} Gets better {"requirement_id":"effort.act
```

The expectation on the dialog (step 10) was written after that round ran. Its red is the card
played alone on the same build of R-02's document and theme, 4 of 4 runs:

```
expect {"path":"pages/promotions.detail/overlays/trade_off/record","not_text":"{\""}
text   Gets worse {"requirement_id":"attention.glance_cost","reference":2,"candidate":3} Gets better {"requirement_id":"effort.
```

Green, R-03: both are met in 4 of 4 runs. The card as a whole stays missed for its last
expectation alone, the state after the accept (UXF-011).

What this does not do:

- A path with a dot is not in the reference of the format: a field's `field` is a name there.
  `ess ui check --model` accepts it (0 errors). A later version of ESS may read it otherwise or
  refuse it; the two expectations above would then fail.
- The journey of `ess/ui/studio.uxui.yaml` requires the nodes `columns/losses` and `columns/gains`.
  In the React app those two are not drawn; their words are at `columns/worse` and
  `columns/better`.
- The members of one record stand in three fields, paired by their place in a row; with two losses
  each field would read `a, b`. Requirement and verdict are paired the same way (UXF-025).
- Every other structured value is still JSON: the budget of a campaign, the first route of a
  variant, the diff of a promotion (maps); the KPI values, the evaluation values and the findings
  of a rule (lists of records). `page_checks.overflow.blocks` is 14, as before.

The same construct would carry the maps in both renderers, since both read a member through an
object: `budget.max_variants` and `budget.max_wall_time_s` as two fields. That was not built: the
budget stands on the live climb, whose terminal drawing is what the golden file
`engine/search/tests/fixtures/studio.frontier.txt` is computed from (one field more in the summary
can change its numbers, and this round does not touch `engine/search`), and its texts are held by a
test and a card that expect the map as one field.

Known loss, as before: the rendering is the stock generator's. Triage unchanged: frequency 6 of 11
scenarios, confidence 2, cost L. Priority 0.82.

## R-04: the maps read in words; the lists of records stay JSON

Built in this round, as R-03's triage named it: every map the studio draws is one field per member,
with a path through an object, which both renderers read alike (`ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml`):

| page | was | is |
|---|---|---|
| the live climb, summary | `budget` as map | `budget.max_variants` "Budget in variants", `budget.max_wall_time_s` "Budget in seconds" |
| a variant, summary; both sides of a comparison | `first_route` as map | `first_route.origin` "Reached by", `first_route.design_space_id` "In design space" |
| a promotion, summary | `diff` as map | `diff.changed` as list, "Changed nodes" |

The first route's `choices` and `applications` are lists of records under the object; they are not
drawn any more (empty in every fixture): drawing them in words needs a path through a list.

Red, the round on the document and theme of R-03 (`R-04/before.round.json`): the new expectations
missed in 4 of 4 runs each, for example

```
expect {"path":"pages/campaigns.climb/sections/summary","not_text":"{\""}
text   target id tg-02 design space id ds-01 strategy beam state Running budget {"max_variants":400,"max_wall_time_s":3600} ...
```

Green, R-04: met in 4 of 4 runs each. `page_checks.overflow.blocks` 14 to 0: all 14 were the budget
(10) and the first route (4).

The terminal test "the budget reads as a map and the KPI values as a list" is restated and renamed
"the budget reads as its members and the KPI values as a list": the budget's two numbers and no
`max_variants` in the summary. It fails on the document of R-03 (step 4) and passes; the journey of
`ess/ui/studio.uxui.yaml` and the constant of `engine/static/tests/main.rs` carry the new name. The
React app draws the seconds as "3,600" (the stock generator groups an integer), the terminal "3600".

Still JSON, and the reason the finding stays open: the lists of records. The KPI values of a frontier
entry (the live climb, the frontier), the values of an evaluation (a variant, a comparison), the
findings of a rule verdict (a variant). Each needs a field path through a list, which ESS has not
decided on (LR-D1); the round adds none and records what it would need in `docs/ess-gaps.md`. The
five paths through an object are not in the format's reference either and hang on the same answer.

Triage: frequency 6 of 11 scenarios as before (the KPI values on live-climb, compare-and-judge,
compare-without-pick, propose-promotion; the evaluations on switch-axis and compare-and-judge),
confidence 2, cost L. Priority 0.82. Known loss for the lists.
