first route, the diff, what gets worse and better, the gate requirement verdicts, the KPI values,
the evaluation values, the findings of a rule. The terminal renderer prints the same values as
"max_variants: 400, max_wall_time_s: 3600".

on the live climb at every viewport, the first route of a variant at 1280x720 and 960x1080.

text"). The studio cannot change it; under triage rule 5 it is a known loss. The studio's handling
of it is in UXF-001 and UXF-002: do not put these values into table columns.
