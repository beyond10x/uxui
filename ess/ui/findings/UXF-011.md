The round measures the app on its fixtures. In that mode the generated app applies the effect of
`StopCampaign` and of no other command the round sends: `StartCampaign`, `RequestAxisSwitch`,
`RecordJudgement` and `ProposePromotion` create a row, `AcceptTradeOff` sets a value fixture mode
does not compute. The notice says so in the generator's words: "uxsearch.promotion.AcceptTradeOff
done in fixture mode; its effect was not applied: its success sets `accepted_by` from a value
fixture mode does not compute".

So the promotion stays Proposed after the Owner accepted the trade-off, no campaign appears after
the start form, no judgement and no promotion appear in their lists. Gate 3 (unmissable completion)
cannot be measured for these five on fixtures, and two scenarios of this round carry no expectation
for that reason.

Input the studio does not control: a known loss under triage rule 5. Its handling is the round's,
not the UI's: a served backend, or fixtures that apply these effects.

Heuristic 14 (unmistakable end state): fail on fixtures; not observed for a real backend.

Triage: frequency 5 of 11 scenarios (owner-trade-off, switch-axis, start-campaign,
compare-and-judge, propose-promotion), confidence 2 (the trial's expectation and its notices
agree), cost L. Priority 0.68. Known loss.
