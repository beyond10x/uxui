The live climb, the frontier, a variant and the comparison of two variants open without a heading.
The first thing on the page is a connection badge or an action. Each of the four declares a
`title` and a `header:` with a live channel or an action; the six pages without a `header:` show
their title. So a declared header drops the page's title in the generated app.

On the live climb and the frontier the switch "Live climb | Frontier" stands in for a title. A
variant and a comparison have nothing: which variant this is stands only in the first field.

Heuristics 2 (state in words) and 10 (recognition over recall): partial and fail.

Triage: frequency 5 of 11 scenarios (live-climb, switch-axis, compare-and-judge,
compare-without-pick, propose-promotion), confidence 2 (the audit and the heuristic review agree),
cost S. Priority 2.73.

Fix in one line: give each of the four headers its title, from the row where the page shows one
thing.

## Closed in R-03

Each of the four headers declares `title: from_page` (`ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml:154`, `:219`, `:250`,
`:297`). The generated app draws the title of a page in its header only when the header names one;
a page without a `header:` draws it itself. The terminal renderer prints the title in every header
either way, so `ess/ui/tests/studio.yaml` could not hold this: the asset is in the round.

The audit bars of R-02 hold the most a cell may show. A title is the least a cell must show, so
`studio.audit.json` has a second set, `floors`, and `summary.mjs` counts the cells under a floor
into `audit.cells_under_a_floor`.

Red, the round on the document of R-02 (`R-03/before.round.json`), the line of `summary.mjs`:

```
summary: floor h1 >= 1: missed, 16 of 40 cells under it, least 0
```

Green, R-03:

```
summary: floor h1 >= 1: held, 0 of 40 cells under it, least 1
```

| | R-02 | the same input, measured again | R-03 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `audit.pages_without_h1` | 4 | 4 | 0 |
| `audit.cells_under_a_floor` | not counted | 16 of 40 | 0 of 40 |

The titles are the pages' own: "Live climb", "Frontier", "Variant", "Compare variants". Which
campaign or which variant the page shows still stands only in its first field: the fix line asked
for the title "from the row where the page shows one thing", and a header takes its title from the
page or from one field of a section's row (`title_from`), not from both. That part is UXF-015's
(labels are field names and values are ids).

A header with a title is 9 px taller than one with only a connection badge. A variant was 709 px of
content and is 718 px now: at 1280x720 it still fits its 720 px, with 2 px left.
