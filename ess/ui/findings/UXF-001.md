The gate decision of a promotion is one table row with six columns, three of them structured values
drawn as JSON (UXF-005). The table is 1797 px wide in a frame of 1042 px at 1366x768 and 956 px at
1280x720, so "Accept trade-off" and "Reject" are outside the frame when the page opens. Nothing but
a faded edge says that the table goes on.

Once the action is reached (the keyboard's first Tab stop on the page, or a sideways scroll), the
frame has moved 755 px and the gate verdict is out of view: the page checks find the document's own
`always` item "gate" clipped at both gating viewports. Verdict and action are never on screen
together.

Heuristic 3 (nothing required hidden): fail.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (owner-trade-off, an anchor card), confidence 2 (the page
checks, the trial and the heuristic review agree), cost M. Priority 0.82.

Fix in one line: show the decision as a record with its actions beside the verdict, not as a wide
table row.
