In R-01 the gate decision of a promotion was one table row with six columns, three of them
structured values drawn as JSON (UXF-005). The table was 1797 px wide in a frame of 1042 px at
1366x768 and 956 px at 1280x720, so "Accept trade-off" and "Reject" were outside the frame when the
page opened. Nothing but a faded edge said that the table went on.

Once the action was reached (the keyboard's first Tab stop on the page, or a sideways scroll), the
frame had moved 755 px and the gate verdict was out of view: the page checks found the document's
own `always` item "gate" clipped at both gating viewports. Verdict and action were never on screen
together.

Heuristic 3 (nothing required hidden): fail.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (owner-trade-off, an anchor card), confidence 2 (the page
checks, the trial and the heuristic review agree), cost M. Priority 0.82.

Fix in one line: show the decision as a record with its actions beside the verdict, not as a wide
table row.

## Closed in R-02, by the yes side of the round's design decision

`ess/ui/rounds/R-02/decision.md` holds the decision: yes is built, and it is the default. An answer
"no" takes the change back and opens this finding again.

The change: the decision section is a collection drawn as a list (`style: list`,
`ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml:372`). The generator then draws the row as labelled fields with the row's
actions beside them, and `themes/studio.css` sets the fields of a list row in columns. Every node
path of the section stays as it was (`rows/<id>/columns/<name>`, `rows/<id>/row_actions/<name>`),
so the tests, the journeys of `ess/ui/studio.uxui.yaml` and the scenario cards did not change. A
list has no column headings: the check "gate" now names the verdict by the end of its path
(`.../columns/gate`), which is the heading in a table and the row's field in a list.
