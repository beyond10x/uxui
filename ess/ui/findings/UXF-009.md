A confirm, a dialog and a drawer open with `role=dialog` and `aria-modal=true`, and focus stays on
the button behind them. The next Tab does reach the dialog's Close button, so stopping a campaign
by keyboard completes (10 key presses against 2 clicks). Escape does nothing; Cancel or Close is
the way out. After the dialog closes, focus is on the document body: a keyboard user starts again
from the top of the page.

The overlays are the generated runtime's, with no focus or Escape handling in it. Input the studio
does not control: a known loss under triage rule 5.

Heuristics 16 (focus follows the work) and 9 (keyboard and pointer parity): fail and partial.

Triage: frequency 6 of 11 scenarios (stop-campaign, stop-campaign-keyboard, dismiss-confirm,
owner-trade-off, compare-and-judge, propose-promotion), confidence 2 (the trial and the heuristic
review agree), cost L. Priority 0.82. Known loss.
