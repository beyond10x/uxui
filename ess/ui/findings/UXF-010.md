After Stop the notice reads "uxsearch.search.StopCampaign done". Every command answers in this
form: the command's id from the specification and "done". The person stopped a campaign; the screen
names a type.

The notice stays 5 s at the bottom right. In fixture mode it is longer and reaches over the edge of
the navigation (UXF-011).

Whether the studio document can set the words of a notice was not checked in this round; the cost
below assumes it cannot do so in one line.

Heuristic 7 (words the user can say): fail.

Triage: frequency 7 of 11 scenarios (stop-campaign, stop-campaign-keyboard, owner-trade-off,
switch-axis, start-campaign, compare-and-judge, propose-promotion), confidence 2 (the trial's
notice texts and the heuristic review agree), cost M. Priority 1.91.

Fix in one line: say what happened to what ("Campaign cp-01 stopped").

## R-03: the asset is there and fails; the studio cannot change the words

Checked in this round, with ess 0.99.2: the words of a notice are not the document's and not the
theme's. The generated runtime writes them in one place (`src/runtime/actions.tsx`, `runCommand`),
from the command's qualified name:

| when | the notice |
|---|---|
| the command succeeded | `<command> done` |
| it succeeded on fixtures and its effect was not applied | `<command> done in fixture mode; its effect was not applied: <reason>` |
| it was sent and the answer is awaited | `<command> sent` |
| it failed | `<command> failed: <reason>` |

Every way the document sends a command ends there: an action's `does`, a confirm, a form. An
`ess-ui/1` action has `label`, `confirm` and `bind`, and a form has `does`; none of them has a key
for what the notice says, and the theme cannot change a text. The fix needs a change in ESS or an
edit of generated output, and the studio does not edit generated output. So this finding is a known
loss (triage rule 5), cost L, and stays open.

The regression asset was written all the same: one expectation for every card, `notices.not_text`
in `studio.scenarios.json`. A run records the notices seen at its moments and those that contain
the text. It is counted beside the run's own expectations and does not change whether the run
completed: a card that completes today would otherwise stop completing for a text the studio cannot
change, and the round would lose sight of everything else the card holds.

The lines of `summary.mjs`, before the changes of the round and after them:

```
summary: no notice contains "uxsearch.": missed, in 26 of 44 runs
summary: no notice contains "uxsearch.": missed, in 28 of 44 runs
```

The two runs more are `switch-axis` at 1280x720: its click lands since UXF-002 closed, so its notice
is shown. Seven of eleven cards send a command, and every one of their runs misses.

Triage: frequency 7 of 11 scenarios, confidence 2, cost L. Priority 0.95. Known loss.

What is missing in the format, in one line: an action, a confirm and a form name the text of the
notice their command answers with, per outcome, with the row's fields at hand
("Campaign cp-01 stopped").
