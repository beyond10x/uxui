Seen on "Start a campaign":

- Submitted with nothing but the target filled, the form answers "done". No field is marked as
  required and none is named as missing.
- The button reads "Save". It starts a campaign.
- It is below the fold: the form is 1084 px of content in a 768 px viewport.
- `strategy` is declared a choice and is drawn as a free text field; `budget`, a map, is one text
  field with no hint of its form.
- `target id` can be edited, though the page was opened on one target.
- The suggestions under journeys, environments and KPIs are ids (`jr-01`, `env-desk`,
  `kpi-effort`) on buttons 21 px high.

Whether an empty start is refused by a real backend was not measured: fixture mode validates
nothing (UXF-011).

Heuristics 6 (errors cheap) and 1 (one next thing): fail and partial.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (start-campaign), confidence 2 (the trial and the heuristic
review agree), cost M. Priority 0.27.

Fix in one line: name the button for what it does, mark what is required, and show the target as
text.
