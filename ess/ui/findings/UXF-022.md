Every live step adds a row to the step table, which is sorted by step number, so the row lands at
the bottom and everything under the table moves down: "Frontier now" moved 78 px between two
moments of one scenario. The table may grow to 200 rows. The person reading the frontier, or about
to press "Open" on one of its rows, has the target move under the pointer.

Measured after load, the page's layout shift is small (0.0135 at most, at 960x1080); the page
checks do not watch a live event.

Heuristic 4 (stable layout): partial.

Triage: frequency 1 of 11 scenarios (live-climb), confidence 2 (two screenshots of one run and the
document's declaration agree), cost M. Priority 0.09.

Fix in one line: give the step table a fixed height with its own newest row in view, or put the
frontier above it.
