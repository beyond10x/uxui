The generated app prints a field's name where the document gives no label. On every page:

- labels: "target id", "design space id", "kpi values", "evaluation keys", "state space key",
  "gate policy revision", "gate requirement verdicts", "insufficient because", "seq", "at";
- badges: `lower_is_better`, `pairwise_preference`; values `space_exhausted`, `bound_hit`;
- ids where a name exists: the target of a campaign is "tg-02", not "Settings page"; a campaign is
  "cp-01"; rules are "rl-density"; axes are "ax-theme";
- the same thing under two names: the page "Promote" lists a promotion; "Watch" opens "Live climb".

Four fields carry a label today ("Host slot", "Trade-off", "Gets worse", "Gets better") and read
well.

Heuristics 7 (words the user can say) and 10 (recognition over recall): fail.

Triage: frequency 11 of 11 scenarios, confidence 1 (the heuristic review alone), cost M. Priority
1.50.

Fix in one line: label every column and field in the person's words, and show names for ids where
the view has them.
