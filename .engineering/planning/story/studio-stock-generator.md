# Story: Studio on the stock generator: fix what the studio document causes

## Outcome

The studio generated by the stock generator shows structured values and ordered rows the way a person
reads them.

## Context

Story 2 of the generator round (`docs/generator-round.md:25-26`); first findings recorded on `ess`
0.98.0 (lines 41-67). F2 (structured values printed as JSON) and the step table's order (F1, second
half) are the studio document's fault and are fixed in `ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml`. The chart's order
(F1) needs ESS (gap 6 of `docs/ess-gaps.md`); F4 is solved in `themes/studio.css`; F5 is
`story:themes-light-dark`.

Shared surface: `ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml` and `ess/ui/tests/studio.yaml`, which
`story:spec-hardening-pass` is changing; this story runs after it (`depends_on`).

## Acceptance

`ess ui test --path ess/ui/studio.ui.yaml --model ess ess/ui/tests/studio.yaml` passes with steps
that read the budget and KPI values as a map or list and the step table's rows in its declared sort
order.

## Out of Scope

The friction ledger (held, `specification-blocker:generator-round-nouns`).
